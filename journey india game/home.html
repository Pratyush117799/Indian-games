Story Mode (Chapter 1 Prototype)
A Pokemon-style pixel RPG of Indian life. Begin as a toddler in a 1990s middle-class home with family, stats, choices, and mini-games.
  • Top-down 2D home with mother, father, and dadi
  • Stats: Health, Happiness, Curiosity, Family Bond
