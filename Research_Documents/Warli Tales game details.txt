## 🎨 Warli Tales — Game Strategy & Architecture Blueprint

---

### 🧠 Core Game Philosophy

Warli art is **minimalist geometry** — circles, triangles, lines. This makes it *perfect* for a stick/shape builder because every Warli figure (human, animal, tree, hut) is composed of just **3 shapes**: ○ △ |. The game isn't about "drawing" — it's about **snapping shapes together to tell a story**, like magnetic poetry but visual and Indian.

The magic happens at the intersection of **creative freedom + guided folktale structure**.

| Mode | Description | Age | Tech |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free Play** | Open sandbox — place any shapes, any arrangement | 5+ | Canvas + Framer |
| **Story Mode** | Follow a folktale chapter by chapter, build each scene | 6+ | Story engine + Audio |

### 🌟 Exclusive Features (Hackathon Differentiators)

**1. 🧲 Magnetic Snap Engine**
Shapes don't just drag — they *snap* together with a satisfying magnetic pull. Circles (heads) automatically centre over triangles (bodies). This prevents frustration for young children and creates authentic Warli proportions.

**2. 🗣️ AI Story Narrator (Claude-powered)**
Child builds a scene → presses "Tell My Story" → Claude API analyses the shapes placed and narrates an original folktale about that scene in Hindi or English. Every child's creation gets a *unique* story.

**3. 🏕️ Scene Intelligence**
The system detects what elements are on canvas (trees + animals = forest, huts + people = village) and **automatically suggests** matching backgrounds and ambient sounds. Drop a tiger → forest sounds fade in.

**4. 🎖️ Warli Artisan Badges**
Progressive achievement system with culturally named ranks: *Shagird (Apprentice)* → *Kalakar (Artist)* → *Chitrakaar (Master Painter)*. Badges are themselves tiny Warli artworks.

**5. 📖 Community Gallery (Class Wall)**
Classroom feature — teacher creates a "wall" and children share their scenes. Gallery displays creations as a collective Warli mural (all scenes stitched together).

**6. 🔊 Tribal Audio Ecosystem**
Authentic Warli/Adivasi ambient sounds (dhol, tarpa wind instrument, forest sounds). Each background has its own soundscape. Shapes make different sounds when placed (water → splash, fire → crackle).

**7. 👆 One-Finger Mode**
For ages 5–7: simplified mode where tapping a shape places it automatically in a pre-defined zone, no dragging required. Reduces motor skill barrier.
