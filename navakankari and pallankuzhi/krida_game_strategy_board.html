Core design philosophy
Zero friction, maximum play
Game loads in under 2 seconds. No login required to play vs AI. One click to start. Text is decoration — the board is the hero. Every pixel serves the game.
Cultural immersion, not museum exhibit
Traditional motifs (rangoli, kolam, mehndi geometry) woven into UI. Sounds of clay pots, dhol, nature. Games feel alive and rooted — not digital artefacts.
Children & elders share the same portal
Font scales, high contrast, simple iconography. Kids see a storybook; scholars see depth. Difficulty unlocks — tutorial mode hides complexity until earned.
Every session leaves a trace
XP, streaks, personal best, replay share link. The game remembers you. Returning feels like coming home — not starting over.
The minimal UI principle
Rule 1
Board occupies 70% of screen
Score, timer, and controls live in a slim strip. The board is never competing for attention. On mobile, it fills the entire viewport.
Rule 2
Text only on hover / tap
Legal moves, scores, and hints appear only when the player interacts. The resting state is pure board — no clutter, no noise.
Rule 3
Animations teach the rules
First-time players watch the game animate itself. Seeds pour from cup to cup. Pieces glide. Rules are felt before they are read.

Identity
Player ID Tag system
Every player auto-assigned a unique tag: #Krida-xxxxxxx. Format: Cultural prefix (Arjun, Kaveri, Dharma…) + 4-digit number. Display on leaderboard, share links, challenge invites. Optional name + avatar later — but the tag gets them playing instantly.
#Arjun-4291#Kaveri-0072#Dharma-1138
Progress
XP & rank ladder
Win vs AI (easy) → +5 XP. Win vs AI (hard) → +20 XP. Win ranked → +40 XP. Daily challenge → +15 XP. Streak day → +10 XP. Ranks: Pebble → Stone → Warrior → Scholar → Sage → Grandmaster.
🪨 Pebble→ ⚔ Warrior→ 📜 Scholar→ 🏆 Sage
Habit
Daily challenge loop
Every day at midnight IST, 3 new challenges appear: one Navakankari puzzle (win in N moves), one Pallankuzhi capture puzzle, one speed round. Completing all 3 grants a flame badge on profile. 7-day streak unlocks exclusive avatar skin.
Social
Challenge & share system
One-click share: generates a short URL with current game state. Friends click to challenge you to the same position. "Replay link" lets you share a full game replay. WhatsApp / Instagram share card auto-generated with your move highlight.

Offline-first hook — what brings players back
Push notifications (PWA)
Daily challenge ready. Your ranked opponent just made a move. Streak expiring in 2 hours. Friend challenged you. Tournament starts tomorrow.
Async multiplayer
Games don't need both players online simultaneously. Make your move, close the app. Opponent gets notified. Games persist for 72 hours.
Weekly tournament
Every Sunday: 8-player Swiss-format bracket. Free to enter with tag ID. Winners featured on the Hub homepage for the entire week.

Online player ID tag — how it works
On first visit, the browser fingerprint + timestamp seeds a tag generator. A cultural name is drawn randomly from a curated list of 200 Indian names (Arjun, Kaveri, Dharma, Priya…) and combined with a 4-digit sequence. The tag is stored in localStorage and the DB simultaneously. No email, no password — just a tag. Players who want to persist across devices can optionally link an email later to claim their tag permanently.
