Detailed Classic Mode Rulebook (Playtesting Ready)
ASTRA: Weapons of the Gods – Classic Mode Rulebook v1.0
Overview
2 players duel using divine and conventional weapons from Indian mythology. Strategy revolves around mantra management, field control, and mythological counters.
Components (for Physical Playtest)

200 cards (print base 100 first).
Life trackers (30 starting each).
Mantra tokens.
Field mats (5 slots per player).

Setup

Each player builds a 40-card deck (max 2 copies of any card).
Shuffle, draw 5 cards.
Starting life: 30.
First player skips first mantra gain.

Turn Structure

Draw Phase: Draw 1 card (max hand 7).
Mantra Phase: Gain 1 mantra (max 10).
Main Phase: Play cards from hand (pay mantra cost). Artifacts stay on field; Spells resolve immediately.
Battle Phase: Attack with one fielded artifact (direct or clash opponent's).
End Phase: Resolve effects, discard to 7.

Combat Resolution

Compare Power (higher wins, deals difference as life damage).
Modifiers:
Higher tier: +2 Power.
Elemental counter: +3 Power.
Specific counteredBy match: Defender wins automatically.

Tie: Both destroyed.
Edge case: Supreme mutual (e.g., Brahmastra vs Brahmastra) → both destroyed, no damage.

Card Types

Artifact (e.g., Gandiva): Stays on field, attacks each turn.
Spell/Astra: One-shot effect.
Support: Buffs/healing.

Win Conditions

Opponent life = 0.
Deck out (milling rare due to draw).

Advanced/Edge Cases

Surrender mechanic (Narayanastra): Opponent may discard hand to negate.
Eternal abilities trigger in graveyard.
Multiplayer variant: 3–4 players, attacks clockwise.

Ready for playtesting — print 60 cards, use tokens, and balance feedback welcome!
3. Clash Mode Balance Sheet + Unit Stats Table
Balance Principles:

Mantra regen: +1 every 2.5s (max 10).
Derived stats: HP = power × 60, Damage = power × 20, Cost = mantraCost.
Roles: Conventional → cheap melee, Elemental → mid-range spells, Celestial → strong units, Supreme → expensive win-cons.
Cycle: Garuda counters Serpent swarms, etc.
