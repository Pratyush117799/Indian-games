# ASTRA: Weapons of the Gods – Master Game Manual & Compendium

## 1. Introduction & Lore
**ASTRA: Weapons of the Gods** is a strategic card-battle game rooted in the epic narratives of Indian mythology (Mahabharata, Ramayana, and the Puranas). Players take on the role of divine warriors invoking legendary weapons—Astras—to duel for cosmic supremacy.

### The World of Astra
From the world-ending **Brahmastra** to the swift **Agneyastra**, each card represents a weapon with its own personality, mythological history, and strategic role. The game balances raw power with precise counterplay, reflecting the divine laws of the universe.

## 2. Core Game Mechanics

### The Four Pillars of Strategy
1.  **Mantra (Resource):** Every action requires Mantra. It is gained sequentially each turn (Classic) or regenerated over time (Clash).
2.  **Power (Strength):** The raw numerical value (1–12) used to determine the outcome of a clash.
3.  **Tiers (Hierarchy):**
    *   **Supreme:** Ultimate god-tier weapons (e.g., Sudarshana Chakra, Pashupatastra).
    *   **Celestial:** Divine armaments (e.g., Vajra, Gandiva).
    *   **Elemental:** Nature-based weapons (Fire, Water, Wind, Earth, Serpent).
    *   **Conventional:** Mortal weapons (Swords, Spears, Bows).
4.  **Counters (The Neutralization Law):** Mythological accuracy dictates that certain weapons automatically neutralize others (e.g., Garudastra counters all Serpent weapons).

### The Counter System
*   **Tier Bonus:** A higher-tier weapon gains **+2 Power** when clashing with a lower-tier weapon.
*   **Elemental Bonus:** Using a weapon that naturally counters the opponent's element (e.g., Water vs. Fire) grants **+3 Power**.
*   **Mythological Override:** If a card explicitly lists a "Counter" relationship, the countering card wins the clash automatically, regardless of Power or Tier.

## 3. Game Modes

### Mode A: Classic Duel (Turn-Based Strategy)
A 1v1 tactical battle where positioning and timing are key.
*   **Deck Size:** 40 cards (max 2 copies per card).
*   **Starting Life:** 30 HP.
*   **Turn Flow:**
    1.  **Draw Phase:** Draw 1 card.
    2.  **Mantra Phase:** Gain 1 Mantra (Cap: 10).
    3.  **Main Phase:** Play Artifacts (stay on field) or Spells (one-shot).
    4.  **Battle Phase:** Attack with one fielded Artifact.
    5.  **End Phase:** Resolve effects; discard to hand size 7.

### Mode B: Clash Battle (Real-Time Lane Warfare)
A fast-paced mobile-first mode where cards become deployable units in a multi-lane battlefield.
*   **Mantra Regen:** +1 every 2.5 seconds.
*   **Stat Conversion:**
    *   **HP:** Power × 60.
    *   **Damage:** Power × 20.
*   **Objective:** Destroy the opponent's "Vimana" (Main Tower).

## 4. Card Types & Rarity

### Card Categories
*   **Artifacts:** Weapons that remain on the field and can attack every turn.
*   **Spells/Astras:** Instant effects that resolve and go to the graveyard.
*   **Support:** Cards that provide healing, mantra boosts, or card draw.

### Rarity Levels
*   **Common / Rare:** The backbone of most decks.
*   **Epic / Legendary:** Powerful mid-to-late game swings.
*   **Mythic:** Extremely rare cards with holographic effects and game-ending abilities.
