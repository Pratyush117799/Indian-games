# Clash Mode Balance Sheet & Unit Stats

## Core Balance Rules
- Mantra regen: +1 every 2.5 seconds (max 10)
- HP ≈ power × 60
- Damage ≈ power × 20
- Cost = mantraCost
- Deployment time varies by tier
