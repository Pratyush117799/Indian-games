# ASTRA: Weapons of the Gods – Classic Mode Rulebook v1.0

## Overview
A 2-player strategic duel using divine and mortal weapons from Indian mythology. Core gameplay revolves around mantra management, field control, and precise mythological counterplay.

## Components
- 200 cards (start playtesting with base 100)
- Life trackers (30 starting)
- Mantra tokens
- Optional field mats (5 slots per side)

## Setup
- Build 40-card decks (max 2 copies of any card)
- Shuffle and draw 5 cards
- Starting life: 30 each
- Coin flip for first player (first player skips initial mantra gain)

## Turn Structure
1. Draw Phase – Draw 1 card (max hand size 7)
2. Mantra Phase – Gain 1 mantra (max 10)
3. Main Phase – Play any number of cards (pay mantra cost)
4. Battle Phase – Attack with one fielded artifact (direct life damage or clash opponent's artifact)
5. End Phase – Resolve lingering effects, discard to hand size 7

## Combat Resolution
- Compare modified Power values
- Modifiers:
  - Higher tier: +2 Power
  - Elemental counter: +3 Power
  - Specific "counteredBy" match: Defender auto-wins
- Winner deals difference as life damage (direct attack) or stays on field
- Tie: Both destroyed

## Card Types
- Artifact (e.g., Gandiva, Trishula): Permanent field presence
- Spell/Astra: One-time effect
- Support: Buffs, draw, healing

## Win Conditions
- Reduce opponent to 0 life
- Opponent cannot draw (deck out)

## Edge Cases
- Mutual Supreme clash → both destroyed, no damage
- Narayanastra → opponent may discard entire hand to negate
- Eternal abilities trigger even from graveyard
