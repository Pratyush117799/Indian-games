# ASTRA: Weapons of the Gods – Card Artwork Prompts (50+ Cards)

## Style Guidelines (Apply to All Cards)
- **Format**: Vertical card composition (portrait orientation, 2:3 ratio ideal for game cards).
- **Dynamic Properties**: Design with digital animation in mind – include glowing auras, particle effects (fire sparks, lightning arcs, swirling illusions), motion trails, energy pulses, and subtle deity silhouettes for premium feel. Holographic foil potential on borders/edges for Mythic/Legendary.
- **Art Style**: Fusion of classical Indian miniature painting (intricate borders, vibrant jewel tones, symbolic motifs like lotuses, serpents, flames, Sanskrit-inspired engravings) with modern epic fantasy (dramatic cinematic lighting, radiant divine glows, high contrast, volumetric god rays).
- **Composition**: Weapon centered in explosive action pose (mid-invocation, unleashing energy). Background: Ethereal cosmic voids, stormy skies, ancient battlefields, or divine realms. Leave space at top for name/title and bottom for stats/flavor text.
- **Color Palette**: Rich golds, crimsons, sapphire blues, emerald greens, with intense divine radiance (glowing edges, lens flares).
- **Resolution**: 1024x1536 or higher for print/digital sharpness.
