Game Log

Welcome, Prince! Choose your weapon and protect the kingdom.

The Prince's Destiny

You are Prince Arjun, heir to the ancient kingdom of Rajgir. Your father, the king, has entrusted you with the sacred duty of protecting the kingdom from the demonic forces that threaten its peace. For years, dark creatures have been emerging from the surrounding forests, attacking villages and terrorizing the people.

Your task is clear: defeat 25 demons, each with 100 hitpoints, to prove your worth as a ruler. Choose your weapon wisely - each has unique strengths and weaknesses. As you defeat demons, you will gain experience and level up, increasing your health and weapon power.

Medicines will appear randomly during battle - use them to restore your health. Remember, you have only one life. If you succeed, you will be crowned king. If you fail, you will be exiled forever.

Part 2: The Demon King's Wrath (Coming Soon!)

After proving your worth by defeating the demon hordes and being crowned king, you will face an even greater challenge. The demon king Mara, enraged by your victory over his minions, will launch a full-scale assault on your kingdom.

Mara has already defeated your army's forward battalions and now marches toward the capital with his elite demon warriors. As the newly crowned king, you must rally your forces, forge alliances with neighboring kingdoms, and develop new strategies to counter Mara's dark magic.

The war is not over yet - in fact, the true battle for the soul of your kingdom is just beginning. Prepare yourself for epic castle sieges, magical battles, and the ultimate confrontation with Mara himself in the upcoming expansion!
