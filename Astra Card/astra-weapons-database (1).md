## BALANCE NOTES

**Power to Cost Ratio:**
- Common: ~3-5 power for 1-2 cost
- Rare: ~5-7 power for 2-3 cost
- Epic: ~7-8 power for 3-4 cost
- Legendary: ~9-11 power for 5-6 cost
- Mythic: ~11-12 power for 6 cost

**Mantra Economy:**
- Turn 1: 3 Mantra (play cheap cards)
- Turn 3: 5 Mantra (Elemental tier accessible)
- Turn 5: 7 Mantra (Celestial plays possible)
- Turn 7: 9 Mantra (Supreme weapons viable)

**Special Abilities Balance:**
- Status effects (Burn, Freeze, Stun): +1 cost equivalent
- Card draw: +1-2 cost
- Multi-target: -2 power per target
- Defense abilities: Cost = damage blocked ÷ 2

## BALANCE NOTES

**Power to Cost Ratio:**
- Common: ~3-5 power for 1-2 cost
- Rare: ~5-7 power for 2-3 cost
- Epic: ~7-8 power for 3-4 cost
- Legendary: ~9-11 power for 5-6 cost
- Mythic: ~11-12 power for 6 cost

**Mantra Economy:**
- Turn 1: 3 Mantra (play cheap cards)
- Turn 3: 5 Mantra (Elemental tier accessible)
- Turn 5: 7 Mantra (Celestial plays possible)
- Turn 7: 9 Mantra (Supreme weapons viable)

**Special Abilities Balance:**
- Status effects (Burn, Freeze, Stun): +1 cost equivalent
- Card draw: +1-2 cost
- Multi-target: -2 power per target
- Defense abilities: Cost = damage blocked ÷ 2
